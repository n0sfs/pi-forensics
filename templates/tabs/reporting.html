Re-hashes every completed acquisition's own output file and compares it against the hashes recorded at acquisition time - the case-wide equivalent of File Explorer's single-file "Verify Image Hash" action. Runs as a background job and uses the one station-wide job slot, so it will block a new acquisition/recovery job from starting until it finishes.
