USB Port -

This station's 2 blue (USB 3.0) ports are permanently evidence-only - this app has no way to write-unlock a drive there, ever, regardless of what's clicked. Only the 2 black (USB 2.0) ports can be write-unlocked (e.g. for a Live Collection USB build's destination drive). Move this drive to a black port to unlock it.
