What populates each section of an exported report, and where to edit that data before you export. The Report Template Builder (Settings > Case & Reporting) lets you choose which sections appear, reorder them, rename them, and - for the text sections marked "Remappable" - choose which of your narrative fields fills that slot. It can't rewire structured sections (tables, logs) to a different source.
